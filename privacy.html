Privacy Policy

AI Yordamchi is a private assistant used by its owner and a few invited users. It is not available to the public. Each user's data is kept separately and is used only to answer that user's own requests.

Google, Instagram and Facebook data

With each user's permission the app accesses that user's own Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Sheets and Drive, Instagram professional account and Facebook Pages only to show, create, change, publish or delete content when the user asks for it. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer or server.

Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. Content needed to answer a request may be processed by the AI provider the owner has chosen (Anthropic) solely to produce that answer.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Users can disconnect any service at any time in the bot (/ulanishlar), delete all their data (/ochirish), or revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and in Instagram / Facebook settings (Apps and websites).
